## 1. Return Eligibility by Reason

| Reason Code | Return Eligibility | Required Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| `damaged` | Eligible | Product arrived physically damaged. Photo evidence may be requested. |
| `not_as_described` | Eligible | Product materially differs from its listing, specifications, or description. |
| `wrong_item` | Eligible | Customer received a different product, model, size, or variant. |
| `no_longer_needed` | Conditional | Product must be unused, unopened, and in original packaging. |
| `defective` | Eligible | Product has a manufacturing defect or does not function as intended. |
| `arrived_late` | Conditional | Delivery occurred after the promised delivery date and the delay affected the customer's purchase. |
| `quality` | Conditional | Product has a demonstrable quality issue that materially affects expected use or performance. |


## 2. Return windows

The delivery date is Day 0. The last eligible day is Day N, ending at 23:59 in the time zone of the delivery address. A request on Day 30 is on time; a request on Day 31 is late.

| Reason | Standard, clothing, furniture | Electronics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `no_longer_needed` | 30 days | 15 days |
| `damaged`, `defective`, `wrong_item`, `not_as_described`, `quality`, `arrived_late` | 30 days | 30 days |

The shorter electronics window applies only to change-of-mind returns. A damaged laptop reported on day 20 is within its window.

### Late requests

| Reason | Handling |
| --- | --- |
| `no_longer_needed` | Deny automatically with a clear explanation. The customer may appeal under Section 11. |
| Every other reason | Escalate to a human reviewer, who may approve or deny the exception. |

## 3. Return Time Windows

| Product / Situation | Return Window |
|---|---|
| Standard products | 30 calendar days |
| Electronics | 15 calendar days |
| Clothing and accessories | 30 calendar days |
| Furniture | 30 calendar days |
| Perishable products | Not eligible for change-of-mind returns |
| Defective products | 30 calendar days from delivery |
| Incorrectly delivered products | 30 calendar days from delivery |

The delivery date is considered Day 0.

A request submitted after the applicable return window is normally ineligible.

Exceptions involving defective products, incorrect deliveries, or other exceptional circumstances must be reviewed by a human reviewer.

## 4. Product Condition Requirements

### 4.1 Unused Products

Unused products are eligible for return when:

- The product is in its original condition.
- All original accessories are included.
- Original packaging is included where applicable.
- The product has no signs of use or damage caused by the customer.

### 4.2 Opened Products

Opened products may be eligible for return if:

- The product has not been used.
- All accessories and components are present.
- The product is not part of a restricted category.

Opening the packaging alone does not automatically make a product ineligible.

### 4.3 Used Products

Products showing normal or excessive usage are generally not eligible for change-of-mind returns.

However, products that become defective during normal use may qualify for a defective-product return.

Such cases must be evaluated separately from ordinary change-of-mind returns.

### 4.4 Damaged Products

Products damaged before delivery or during shipping are eligible for return or replacement.

The customer should provide:

- Photographs showing the damaged product.
- Photographs of the original packaging when available.
- A description of the observed damage.

Missing photographic evidence must not automatically result in rejection. Cases with insufficient evidence should be escalated for review.

Damage caused by customer misuse or accidental damage after delivery is not covered by this policy.

## 5. Non-Returnable Products

The following product categories are generally non-returnable:

1. Perishable goods, including fresh food.
2. Personalized or customized products.
3. Digital products after successful delivery or activation.
4. Products explicitly marked as final sale.
5. Products that cannot be resold for hygiene or safety reasons after being opened.
6. Gift cards and prepaid vouchers.

### Exceptions

The non-returnable restriction does not automatically apply when:

- The product was defective upon delivery.
- The wrong product was delivered.
- The product arrived damaged.
- The product was materially different from its description.

These situations require individual evaluation.

## 6. Refund Eligibility

A customer may receive a refund when:

1. The return request is approved.
2. The product satisfies the applicable return conditions.
3. The returned product is received and inspected when physical inspection is required.
4. The refund amount is calculated according to the original purchase transaction.

Refunds should generally be issued through the original payment method.

### Refund Amount Calculation

The refund amount must not exceed the amount originally paid for the returned item.

The calculation must consider:

- Original item price.
- Applicable discounts.
- Promotional adjustments.
- Applicable taxes.
- Previously issued partial refunds.

Shipping charges are generally non-refundable for change-of-mind returns.

Shipping charges may be refunded when the return is caused by an incorrect delivery, defective product, or shipping damage.

The refund calculation must be performed using transaction data rather than an LLM-generated estimate.

## 7. Refund Processing Time

Once a return is approved and all required inspection steps are completed:

| Payment Method | Expected Processing Time |
|---|---|
| Credit / Debit Card | 5–10 business days |
| Digital Wallet | 3–7 business days |
| Bank Transfer | 5–10 business days |
| Store Credit | 1–2 business days |

These are estimated processing times. Actual crediting time may depend on the payment provider or financial institution.

## 8. Wrong Item Delivered

If a customer receives an item different from the one ordered:

1. The customer is eligible to request a return or replacement.
2. The customer should provide the received product's photographs.
3. The order details must be checked against the delivered product.
4. Return shipping costs must be covered by the retailer.
5. The customer should not be charged a restocking fee.

If the order and product information cannot be reconciled, the request must be escalated.

## 9. Defective Product Returns

A product is considered defective when it fails to perform its intended function under normal usage conditions.

Examples include:

- Electronics that do not power on.
- Products with manufacturing defects.
- Missing essential components.
- Products with functional failures upon delivery.

A customer may request a refund or replacement within 30 calendar days of delivery.

The retailer may request reasonable supporting evidence, such as photographs or a description of the issue.

Cases involving disputed defects, potential misuse, or conflicting evidence must be escalated to a human reviewer.

## 10. Return Shipping and Restocking Fees

### 10.1 Change-of-Mind Returns

For ordinary change-of-mind returns:

- The customer is responsible for return shipping costs.
- A restocking fee of up to 10% may apply to eligible product categories when explicitly disclosed at purchase.
- The fee must not be applied automatically without confirming that the product and transaction qualify.

### 10.2 Retailer-Fault Returns

The retailer covers return shipping when:

- The wrong product was delivered.
- The product arrived damaged.
- The product was defective upon delivery.
- The retailer made a fulfillment error.

No restocking fee applies to these cases.

## 11. Suspicious and Potentially Fraudulent Returns

Return requests must be evaluated for potential abuse.

The following patterns may indicate suspicious return behavior:

1. Repeated return requests from the same account.
2. Repeated claims of damaged or missing products.
3. High-value refund requests with insufficient evidence.
4. Inconsistencies between the customer's description and submitted photographs.
5. Evidence of possible product substitution.
6. Repeated requests involving different accounts but related order or transaction patterns.
7. Unusual return frequency compared with normal purchasing activity.

### Important Fraud Assessment Rules

- A high return rate alone does not prove fraudulent activity.
- An account's age alone does not establish fraud.
- A suspicious photograph alone does not prove that the customer committed fraud.
- Missing image authenticity metadata does not establish that an image is genuine or manipulated.
- Automated fraud indicators must be treated as risk signals rather than definitive evidence.

Suspicious cases must be routed for human review.

The system must not automatically accuse a customer of fraud.

## 12. Human Review and Escalation

The following situations require human review:

| Situation | Required Action |
|---|---|
| Return request submitted after the deadline | Human review |
| Product condition is disputed | Human review |
| High-value refund above $500 | Human review |
| Suspicious return behavior | Human review |
| Potential product substitution | Human review |
| Missing or conflicting evidence | Human review |
| Non-returnable product with a defect claim | Human review |
| Refund calculation cannot be verified | Human review |
| Policy rules conflict | Human review |
| Unusual exception request | Human review |

The $500 threshold is a sample operational threshold and must be configurable.

A human reviewer has the authority to approve or deny exceptions after evaluating the evidence and applicable policy.

## 13. Duplicate Return Requests

A customer cannot receive multiple refunds for the same order item.

If an existing return request is already approved or completed, a new request for the same order item must be blocked from automatic processing.

If an existing request is pending, the new request should be linked to the existing case or escalated for review.

A previously denied return may be reconsidered only when new relevant evidence or information is provided.

## 14. Partial Refunds

Partial refunds may be issued when:

- Only some items in an order are returned.
- A product has a minor defect that does not prevent its use.
- A partial refund is explicitly approved by a human reviewer.
- A transaction requires adjustment based on a verified pricing discrepancy.

Partial refunds must be calculated from verified order and payment records.

The AI system must never independently invent or negotiate a refund amount.

## 15. Policy Enforcement and Decision Authority

All automated return decisions must follow the following principles:

1. Policy eligibility must be evaluated before refund approval.
2. Verified order and payment data must take precedence over customer-provided assumptions.
3. Missing information must not be treated as evidence of policy violation.
4. Conflicting evidence must trigger human review.
5. Fraud risk indicators must not independently justify rejection.
6. Refund amounts must be calculated using deterministic business logic.
7. Policy exceptions must be recorded with an explanation and reviewer identity.
8. Every automated decision must retain the policy version used during evaluation.

## 16. Policy Versioning

Each policy document must have a unique document ID and version.

When a policy changes:

- A new version must be created.
- The effective date must be recorded.
- Previous versions must remain available for historical decisions.
- Each return decision must record the policy version used.

A return request must be evaluated against the applicable policy version determined by the business's configured policy-effective-date rules.
